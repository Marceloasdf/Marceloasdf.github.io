F-01-69

His ability triggers when his Qliphoth Counter reaches 0
or by doing a 'Request/Commission.' His Qliphoth Counter can fluctuate in the following ways:

  • Decrease by 1 when an Agent with Justice lower than Level 3 finishes work.
  • Decrease by 1 when getting a Normal or Bad Work Result.

Unlike what his Managerial Guidelines say, Der Freischütz's
Qliphoth Counter has a 100% chance of lowering if the work result isn't Good

When Der Freischütz's Qliphoth Counter reaches zero or you
Request/Commission the Abnormality for a shot, he will perform his ability.
